- Aesthetic Principles in Chinese Music
	- Deeply linked to philosophy
	- Confucianism/Daoism
- Pentatonic Scale
	- Gong (Emporer)
	- Shang (Minister)
	- Jiao (People)
	- zhi (National affairs)
	- Yu (material objects)
	- These names are identifiers of importance, not solfege
- Classification of (Traditional) Instruments
	- Ba yin (eight sounds), based on construction material
		- Stone, earth, bamboo, metal, skin, silk, wood, and gourd
		- Note: silk is in the strings in traditional instruments, not in the body
		- Eight is a considered a lucky number in Chinese culture/mythos
- Notation System
	- Transmission was originally oral
	- Often anonymous authors
	- Improvisation: embellishments, dynamics, changes in tempo
	- Development of written notation came later (Gongche)
	- Later iterations were more western (Jianpu)
		- Fun fact: The notation for Jianpu was invented by [Jean-Jacques Rousseau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Jacques_Rousseau) (co-opted into the system)
- Instruments
	- Qin (Guqin)
		- Most highly regarded because of antiquty and rich legacy (associations with educated upper-class)
		- Timeline
			1. Accompanied poetry education
			2. Solo instrument, accompanied chamber vocals
			3. Status enhanced as solo instrument
			4. Restricted to court circles only
			5. Modern: act of contemplation, self-purification, and self regulation (should be played in private amidst charming scenery and incense)
		- Zhiyin: soulmate
		- Made from hollowed board
		- 7 strings
		- Lacquer painted
		- 13 studs
		- C-D-F-G-A-c-d
		- Flat underside often personalized with the name of the owner/instrument
		- Playing involves plucking and stopping with 4 fingers
		- Allows for lots of ornamentation/expression
	- Erhu
		- Means "2", is a 2 string instrument
		- Allegedly from Eurasian nomadic tribess living beyond chinese borders
		- Bow: bamboo and horsetail hair
			- Clipped between the two strings
		- Soundbox covered in python skin
		- Similar technique to python skin
		- Can make a characteristic "neighing" sound
		- Solo and ensemble instrument
		- Fingers do not touch the board when pressing the strings
	- Dizi
		- Bamboo flute
			- Constructed from one continuous piece originally, now constructed of multiple pieces
		- Addition of membrane hole enhanced vibrations
		- Change of distance of fingering holes
		- Half-hole fingering technique
		- Construction: blow hole, membrane hole, 6 fingering holes, a few tuning holes
		- Held to the right (transverse)
		- Can do glide, gliss, trill, vibrato, flutter-tongue, half-hole tuning, and harmonics
- Vocal
	- More nasal than Western
	- Very [melismatic](https://www.britannica.com/art/melisma)
-  Jiangnan Sizhu Ensemble
	- Teahouse music
	- Informal music
	- Silk and Bamboo instrumental tradition
	- Used to be played at weddings and local opera
		- Now better known as amateur music
	- Functional music
